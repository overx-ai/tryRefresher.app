# Off-site posts: Refresher

Playbook and log for answers placed on other platforms. Drafted by `/seo offsite refresher [platform]`. Research and sources: `~/.claude/skills/seo/references/geo.md`. Agreed 2026-11-23 (plan: off-site SEO and GEO for Refresher and ffly).

## Rules
- Nothing goes out without passing `seo/banned.md`, `seo/product.md` and the judge (80). No health outcome claims, ever (no "fix your sleep", "ease anxiety", "instant calm"), in titles, captions or descriptions.
- Every post answers the query in its first line and carries the query's words.
- Brand accounts never name the founder. Our own accounts never like, comment on or share each other.
- Communities (Reddit, Facebook groups) get answers to real questions only, with "I make Refresher" disclosed, and at most one link in ten answers. Anxiety and health communities: describe the pattern and the timing, never what it does for a condition.
- Never paste the same text in two places.
- Publishing needs the owner's yes per batch. The live URL goes back into the log.
